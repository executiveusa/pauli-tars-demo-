This is a mission-invoked Impact District workflow. It is deliberately **not** an always-on skill.
Use it only when the active project is a nonprofit or social-purpose organization and the mission is about donor cultivation, funder research, grant readiness, a case for funding, campaign preparation, stewardship, or fundraising-system design.

Do NOT use it for general prospect audits (use Client Presence Audit), ordinary competitor research, mass cold outreach, automatic donor asks, generic marketing, or speculative wealth profiling. Never send an email, place a call, submit an application, publish an appeal, or make a financial commitment without the applicable owner approval.

## Operating principle

Funding follows demonstrated competence plus trust. Package real proof, cultivate relationships before asking, and frame money as the outcome it unlocks rather than an organizational deficit. Urgency is not evidence of readiness.

## ICM workflow

### 1. CONTEXT — competence pack

Before planning outreach, build one source-backed record of what the organization can already prove:

- who it serves;
- what problem it addresses;
- what has actually changed because it exists;
- dated outcomes, milestones, partnerships, contracts, program evidence, photos/documents, and fiscal-sponsor or legal context when relevant;
- what is still unknown.

Every load-bearing claim must point to a source, artifact, or explicit authorized human statement. Scattered proof is consolidated; missing proof stays missing. Do not turn aspiration into accomplishment.

Output: `competence-pack` + evidence references + confidence/coverage notes.

### 2. RELATIONSHIPS — warm network / 3C map

Build a small relationship map before searching for strangers. For each authorized contact record only:

`Name | Care evidence | Consistency evidence | Capacity signal | Relationship owner | Next touch`

Use the 3C lens:

- **Care** — demonstrated emotional/mission connection or real engagement;
- **Consistency** — repeated support, participation, introductions, advice, in-kind help, or giving;
- **Capacity** — only explicit, public, organizational, or owner-authorized signals that make a larger contribution plausible.

The 3C score is a **conversation-priority signal**, never permission to ask for money. Do not infer sensitive personal financial circumstances or perform invasive wealth profiling.

Output: `warm-network` with evidence and next-touch status.

### 3. CULTIVATION — three touches before an ask

For a relationship that is ready for cultivation, stage the sequence:

1. **Thank-you / appreciation** — no ask. Draft a short personal acknowledgement tied to something real.
2. **Witness the work** — prepare an invitation or evidence experience: visit, walkthrough, project artifact, program demonstration, or concise visual proof.
3. **Ask for advice** — invite perspective on a specific decision or expansion rather than asking for money.

Create drafts, briefs, and recommended timing. Outbound calls/messages remain approval-gated. Record what the person actually responded to before proposing an ask.

Output: `cultivation-plan` with touch history, next action, and owner approval boundary.

### 4. FUNDERS — local and relationship-first map

Research plausible funders only after the competence pack and warm-network map exist. Prioritize:

1. community/regional foundations;
2. local business/corporate giving programs;
3. fiscal-sponsor and partner introductions;
4. employee matching and locally relevant corporate programs;
5. larger institutional funders where eligibility is actually supported.

For every candidate record:

`Funder | Geography | Program fit | Eligibility evidence | Relationship path | Deadline | Evidence source | Status`

Ten plausible warm/relationship-accessible funders beat one hundred blind applications. Never claim eligibility from a program title alone; read the current rules.

Output: `funder-map` with current-source evidence.

### 5. CASE — one-page case for funding

Draft one source document answering only:

1. **Who do we help?**
2. **What problem do they face?**
3. **What changes because we exist?**

Then state the next fundable outcome as an unlock:

`By <date>, <specific people/program outcome> becomes possible. <amount/budget if verified> completes <specific work>.`

Operational budget detail may follow, but the headline is the human/program outcome, not a deficit. Never invent dates, participant counts, budgets, or impact figures.

Before using this as fundraising copy, route the draft to 2–3 appropriate warm stakeholders for **feedback**, not an ask, after owner approval for outreach.

Output: `case-for-funding` + evidence links + unresolved claims.

### 6. CAMPAIGN — one focused campaign

Run one campaign at a time per active fundraising objective.

A campaign must fit in one sentence and define:

- one outcome;
- one audience;
- one verified funding target or non-monetary goal;
- start/end dates;
- evidence assets;
- channel plan;
- approval boundary;
- metrics.

If the campaign cannot be described in one sentence, reduce scope before creating assets.

Output: `campaign-brief`; Creative and Production districts may be dispatched for copy, design, landing page, video, or donation-flow work.

### 7. MAJOR RELATIONSHIPS — 90-day plan

From the warm network, choose at most three relationships that have evidence of strong fit and plausible larger support. Build a 90-day relationship plan based on genuine contact history, not pressure tactics.

No automatic ask is allowed. A proposed ask must be supported by the prior touches and returned to the owner as `NEEDS_YOU` with context, recommended amount/range only when evidence supports it, purpose, and relationship risk.

Output: `90-day-major-relationship-plan`.

### 8. STEWARDSHIP — monthly meaningful touch

For active donors/supporters/partners, recommend roughly one meaningful, evidence-backed touchpoint per month unless the relationship record calls for a different cadence:

- concrete impact update;
- beneficiary/program story with proper consent;
- behind-the-scenes proof;
- volunteer/partner spotlight;
- milestone or lesson learned.

Do not manufacture activity to satisfy a calendar. If there is no meaningful update, record `no meaningful update` instead of sending filler.

Output: `stewardship-queue` — drafts only until approved.

## Cross-district routing

This workflow belongs to the **Impact District** but should delegate rather than duplicate expertise:

- **Intelligence** — current funder research, program eligibility evidence, change monitoring;
- **Revenue** — prospect prioritization, opportunity sizing, verified economics;
- **Creative** — case narrative, campaign copy, visuals, impact storytelling;
- **Production** — donation/volunteer funnel, landing pages, data tools;
- **Commerce** — payment/distribution connectors where governed;
- **Experiment** — message/funnel tests only after a baseline and explicit metric exist;
- **Operations** — approved schedules, reminders, follow-up queue;
- **Command / Heisenberg** — mission decomposition, evidence reconciliation, approvals.

## Completion contract

A fundraising-readiness mission is complete only when the requested artifacts exist with evidence and explicit unknowns. Completion is not an email sent, a grant submitted, or money received unless the mission explicitly requested that consequential action and the canonical approval/evidence path proves it occurred.

Minimum durable outputs when the full workflow is requested:

- competence pack;
- warm-network / 3C map;
- funder map;
- one-page case for funding;
- one focused campaign brief;
- 90-day cultivation plan;
- stewardship queue;
- metrics + next review date.

Stop and return `NEEDS_YOU` when the next step is an external ask, application submission, public campaign, financial commitment, use of sensitive relationship data, or another consequential action.
