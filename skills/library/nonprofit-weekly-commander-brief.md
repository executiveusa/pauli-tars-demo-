Use on a recurring schedule after at least one baseline audit exists.

## Workflow

1. Read the prior weekly brief, active nonprofit-growth missions, evidence receipts, funding pipeline, deadlines, blockers, and audit deltas.
2. Report only state StarNet can prove. A planned task is not complete; a drafted application is not submitted; a discovered grant is not eligible until screened.
3. Group work into `completed-with-proof`, `in-progress`, `blocked-human`, `blocked-system`, `new-opportunity`, and `watch-next`.
4. For each completed item attach the strongest available evidence receipt. For each blocked-human item create one concise Task Brief with decision, recommendation, alternatives, consequence, exact human action, and resume point.
5. Rank the next five actions by expected impact, urgency, effort, and dependency.
6. Include upcoming funding/application dates and any change in digital-trust or funding-readiness status.
7. Continue independent work while awaiting human decisions; do not turn every ambiguity into a meeting request.

## Output

- one-page commander brief
- top five next actions
- human decision queue
- deadlines within the next 30 days
- evidence links for completed work
- risks or contradictions requiring attention

## Definition of done

The owner can understand what changed, what matters next, and what requires a decision in under five minutes, with every completion claim traceable to evidence.
