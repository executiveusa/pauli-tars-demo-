Use this before drafting any grant application.

## Workflow

1. Collect the current opportunity from a primary or authoritative source whenever possible.
2. Capture funder, program, deadline, geography, eligible entity types, fiscal-sponsor rules, award range, match requirements, restricted uses, reporting burden, required attachments, and contact/process details.
3. Compare each requirement against the organization's verified truth set and funding-readiness dossier.
4. Mark each requirement `met`, `likely-met-needs-proof`, `not-met`, `unknown`, or `human-decision-required`.
5. Verify time-sensitive legal, tax, financial, and platform claims rather than trusting a video, blog, or prior application.
6. Score fit using mission alignment, eligibility certainty, award usefulness, effort, deadline pressure, relationship value, reporting burden, and strategic relevance. Do not express the score as a probability of winning unless a defensible model exists.
7. Reject or park clear non-fits early. For viable opportunities, create an application-prep mission with owners, evidence requirements, approval gates, and due dates.

## Human approval gates

Require human review for final eligibility interpretation when ambiguous, certifications, signatures, representations of legal/tax status, budgets that commit funds, and final submission.

## Output

- grant-fit scorecard
- eligibility matrix
- missing-evidence list
- go / park / no-go recommendation
- application-prep mission if viable

## Definition of done

The opportunity has a source-traced eligibility decision and the team can explain why time should or should not be invested before drafting begins.
